Eastwood Global // FAQ

Frequently asked questions

(05)
01 Is Eastwood Global allowed to offer the IB Diploma online?
Yes. Eastwood Global is allowed by the International Baccalaureate to offer the full IB Diploma Programme online, as part of the IB’s approved online model. It is one of seven schools in the world with this permission.
02 What if my child misses a live class?
Every class is live and teacher-led, and every live lesson is recorded. Students who miss a class for training, competition or travel can catch up on the recording and stay level with their peers.
03 What does the admissions process involve?
Applicants to IBDP 1 complete a proctored online assessment in English and Mathematics of around 1.5 hours, followed by an interview with the Admissions and Academic team. Families can expect a decision within approximately two weeks of a complete application.
04 When can my child start the IB Diploma?
Students should apply for the full two-year IB Diploma Programme, starting at the beginning of the academic year. Applications are encouraged by mid-August. Mid-programme entry is restricted.
05 How is tuition shared?
Tuition varies by programme and is shared directly with families during the admissions process. A free admissions call is the fastest way to receive the figures for your child.
